# FocusFrame — Smart Eyewear for the Digital Age

A premium, cinematic single-page product website for FocusFrame smart eyewear — adaptive blue-light blocking glasses with silent touch notification controls.

## Project Overview

FocusFrame is smart eyewear designed for the modern digital lifestyle. This website is a highly persuasive, immersive, premium brand landing page that emotionally sells eye protection, focus, and digital wellness.

### Key Sections

1. **Hero** — Full-screen cinematic intro with floating 3D glasses render, holographic UI elements, and ambient particle effects
2. **The Problem** — Visually intense section highlighting digital overload: blue light, notifications, eye strain, sleep disruption
3. **Transformation** — Animated product showcase demonstrating blue-light filtering, transition lenses, and touch controls
4. **Features** — 6 premium feature cards with hover effects (adaptive lenses, blue-light protection, transition mode, touch controls, lightweight design, battery life)
5. **Lifestyle Environments** — Immersive ambient scenes (workspace, gaming room, outdoor café) with mood lighting
6. **Product Showcase** — Close-up detail views of lens coating, titanium frame, touch sensor, and precision hinges
7. **How It Works** — 4-step animated diagram explaining ambient sensing, adaptive filtering, touch intelligence, and optimization
8. **Psychological Conversion** — Emotional messaging reinforcing the need for FocusFrame
9. **Customization** — Interactive frame color picker (Matte Black, Deep Navy, Silver, Amber) and environment toggles
10. **FAQ** — Expandable accordion with product questions
11. **Comparison** — Feature comparison table vs standard blue-light glasses
12. **Final CTA** — Cinematic closing with warm lighting transition and purchase call-to-action

## Design System

- **Color Palette**: Matte black (#080c14), Deep navy (#0a1628), Warm amber (#f59e0b), Electric cyan (#06b6d4), Indigo (#6366f1)
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk (headings), Inter (body)
- **Visual Style**: Dark futuristic minimalism, glassmorphism UI, cinematic lighting, metallic reflections

## Technology

- **HTML5** — Semantic structure
- **CSS3** — Custom properties, CSS Grid, Flexbox, glassmorphism, animations, 3D transforms
- **JavaScript** — Canvas particle system, Intersection Observer scroll animations, interactive customization, FAQ accordion

## Design Philosophy

Inspired by Apple, Tesla, and Nothing — ultra-premium minimalism with dark futuristic aesthetic. The experience is designed to feel addictive, immersive, elegant, and emotionally persuasive — making visitors feel that FocusFrame is not just a product but an essential upgrade for modern life.
